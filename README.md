# kyrgyzstan-trade-analysis# Kyrgyzstan's Trade Structure and Export Potential

## Overview

This project contains the data, analysis, visualizations, and thesis related to:

**"Kyrgyzstan's Trade Structure and Export Potential: A Comparison with the EU"**

The study analyses the development and structure of Kyrgyzstan's merchandise trade during **2015–2025**, with particular attention to trade partners, product structure, trade concentration, revealed comparative advantage, export diversification, and trade relations with the European Union.

The project combines quantitative analysis in Python with data visualization in Power BI.

---

## Research Objectives

The main objectives of the study are to:

- examine the development of Kyrgyzstan's exports and imports from 2015 to 2025;
- identify Kyrgyzstan's main trading partners;
- analyse the main export and import product groups;
- measure trade concentration using the Herfindahl–Hirschman Index (HHI);
- examine revealed comparative advantage (RCA);
- assess the structure of Kyrgyzstan–EU trade;
- analyse export and import trends using exploratory regression;
- evaluate the opportunities and challenges for export diversification.

---

## Research Period

The empirical analysis covers the period:

**2015–2025**

The analysis uses annual trade data.

---

## Main Research Problems

The project focuses on several important problems affecting Kyrgyzstan's trade structure:

### 1. Import dependence

Kyrgyzstan's imports have grown substantially faster than exports, resulting in a persistent trade deficit.

### 2. Export concentration

A significant share of exports is concentrated in a relatively small number of product groups, particularly mineral and precious-metal products.

### 3. Limited diversification

The country's export structure remains relatively concentrated, which can increase exposure to commodity prices and external market changes.

### 4. Dependence on major trading partners

Trade is concentrated among several major partners, particularly in regional and Asian markets.

### 5. Asymmetric EU trade

Trade with the European Union is strongly asymmetric, with EU exports to Kyrgyzstan substantially exceeding Kyrgyzstan's exports to the EU.

### 6. Export development constraints

Increasing exports requires not only comparative advantage but also stronger production capacity, product diversification, market access, standards compliance, and international competitiveness.

## Methodology

The project uses quantitative and descriptive methods, including:

- descriptive statistics;
- export and import growth analysis;
- trade balance analysis;
- trading-partner analysis;
- product-structure analysis;
- Herfindahl–Hirschman Index (HHI);
- Revealed Comparative Advantage (RCA);
- EU trade comparison;
- exploratory linear regression.

Because the analysis contains only **11 annual observations**, the regression analysis is interpreted as exploratory and does not establish causal relationships.

---

## Data

The project uses international and official trade data, including:

- UN Comtrade;
- World Integrated Trade Solution (WITS);
- Eurostat / European Commission;
- official Kyrgyz statistical sources.

The main Kyrgyzstan trade datasets contain annual export and import values in **million USD**.

EU trade data are presented separately in **million EUR** where the original European Commission/Eurostat data are reported in EUR.
